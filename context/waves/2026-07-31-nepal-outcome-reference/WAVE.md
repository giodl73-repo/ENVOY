# Nepal outcome-reference attribution gate

## Outcome

Make observed national-system outcomes executable without confusing temporal
movement with an MCC Nepal Compact effect.

## Delivered

- checksum-custodied official open-data fixture with seven selected measures;
- deterministic baseline-to-observation arithmetic;
- explicit counts of compact-specific and target-ready rows;
- held Taxlane pack with a concrete attribution reopening test; and
- README, reader, source registry, tests, and role-review fixed point.

## Result

Seven aggregate measures are observed. Zero are compact-specific and zero have
a ready compact target in the selected rows. Outcome-reference readiness is
true; candidate-effect, floors, delivery, savings, admission, and rates remain
false or null.
