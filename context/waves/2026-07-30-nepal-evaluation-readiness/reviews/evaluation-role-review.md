# Evaluation-readiness role review

- `foreign-assistance-methodologist`: **pass**. Procurement status is separated
  from design, collection, completed evaluation, and attributable effect.
- `affected-local-community`: **defer**. No subgroup benefit/burden incidence or
  community-experience evidence is present. Closure: publish and execute the
  local-incidence component for the exposed cohort.
- `do-no-harm-human-rights`: **defer**. No matched safeguard, grievance, or harm
  result is present. Closure: link evaluation results to public aggregate
  safeguard and grievance outcomes without exposing people or sources.
- `citation-auditor`: **pass**. Procurement facts are source-registered and all
  absent design/result fields remain null.
- `numeracy-checker`: **pass**. Forecast value bands remain bands in thousands
  of dollars; they are not treated as project cost, benefit, or savings.

No critical or major finding is promoted. Deferrals are retained as candidate
admission gates, with explicit closure conditions.
