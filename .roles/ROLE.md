# ENVOY role index

Roles return pass, finding, or defer. Critical and major findings block promotion. Dissent remains recorded.

## Parliament

- [career-diplomat](parliament/career-diplomat.md) — Protects diplomatic purpose, context, and durable relationships.
- [development-humanitarian-practitioner](parliament/development-humanitarian-practitioner.md) — Protects delivery quality and humanitarian principles.
- [security-assistance-oversight-lead](parliament/security-assistance-oversight-lead.md) — Protects lawful use, oversight, and diversion controls.
- [regional-local-partner-representative](parliament/regional-local-partner-representative.md) — Protects local ownership, incidence, and context.
- [international-economist](parliament/international-economist.md) — Tracks resource flows, incidence, additionality, and distortion.
- [crisis-logistics-response-officer](parliament/crisis-logistics-response-officer.md) — Tests surge, access, delivery, and disruption.
- [treaty-law-human-rights-counsel](parliament/treaty-law-human-rights-counsel.md) — Protects legal obligations, rights, and do-no-harm.
