# ENVOY product plan

## Outcome

Help analysts test whether international-affairs resources become credible,
locally grounded, durable outcomes without increasing diversion, harm,
dependency, security exposure, or hidden burden shifting.

## Product-first sequence

1. Assistance-realization screen — implemented.
2. Official obligation/disbursement and performance-method source pack.
3. One bounded, non-sensitive program portfolio candidate — implemented as
   the held MCC Nepal Compact delivery envelope.
4. Local incidence, delivery, diversion, and durability audit.
5. Independently reviewed held Taxlane pack.

## Non-goals

Sensitive sources, operations, country rankings, diplomatic or award decisions,
partner targeting, booked savings, allocation, or tax-rate changes.
