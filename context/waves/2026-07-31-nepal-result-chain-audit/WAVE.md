# Nepal complete result-chain audit

## Outcome

Audit every public Nepal ITT row and determine whether any outcome contains a
complete baseline-target-observation chain.

## Delivered

- complete 186-row readiness census by indicator level;
- baseline, target, actual, and full-join counts;
- compact-specific observation count;
- cross-vintage road-design trajectory; and
- deterministic audit and held-pack commands with role review.

## Result

Only one row joins baseline, target, and observation, and it is process-level.
Zero outcome rows form a complete chain and zero explicit MCC-cross-border rows
have an actual. Road design advances from 40 to 76 km, but effects, floors,
savings, admission, and rates remain held.
