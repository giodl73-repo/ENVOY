# Nepal Compact candidate-envelope wave

Outcome: make one active international-assistance delivery chain replayable
from commitment through public implementation milestones without treating
unexpended obligations, contracts, projected benefits, or partner funding as
U.S. savings or achieved outcomes.

## Delivered

- A dedicated `envoy-nepal-compact` feature crate reconciles U.S. compact
  funding, Nepal cofunding, statutory funding components, and expenditures.
- `candidate-baseline` exposes $550M U.S. obligations, $115.8M expenditures,
  $434.2M unexpended obligations, and the separate $197M Nepal contribution.
- `candidate-held-pack` supplies the country, program, local implementing
  entity, MCC oversight, five-year schedule, 297 contracted transmission
  kilometers, three mobilized substations, and explicit overlap rules.
- Completed outputs, durable outcomes, local incidence, safeguards, diversion,
  partner burden, and savings remain unclaimed.

Level 1 candidate-envelope acceptance is complete. Level 2 admissibility is
held pending [candidate-role-review.md](reviews/candidate-role-review.md).
